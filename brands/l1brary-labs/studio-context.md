# Studio context

L1brary Labs is Maurice Thomas's applied AI and emerging technology venture studio.
Public website: https://l1brarylabs.xyz/
Founder portfolio: https://www.mauricethomas.online/
GitHub: https://github.com/dktrn9ne

The website describes independent applied research, real-world engineering, and a venture
process: Research → Validate → Pilot → Venture. Culture supplies human context; engineering
makes a hypothesis testable; commercial validation supports the next commitment.

Public site as observed October 29, 2026:
- Cadence: commercialization initiative; an ongoing product and founder-reported pilot
  opportunity, not proof of a completed or paid pilot.
- ProofRail / GrokDoc: applied AI research on evaluating, monitoring and improving autonomous systems.
- Nullmark: financial technology research on AI-assisted credit dispute workflows and verifiable evidence.
- GridOS and creator tools: studio project context supplied for this configuration; ask for a
  current brief, real UI and validated claims before describing capabilities or readiness.

Never infer a client, sponsor or commercial partnership from hackathon participation or a
technology-provider listing. Project rights and readiness need explicit evidence. Context can
age: use the requester's current verified brief over historical website descriptions.
